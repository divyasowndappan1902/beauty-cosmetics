Engineered in our Grasse laboratory, this multi-depth cellular elixir harnesses the revitalizing power of cold-pressed organic Damascus roses. Blended with low, medium, and high molecular weight hyaluronic acids, it saturates both superficial stratum corneum layers and deeper cellular junctions with weightless, sustained moisture.
